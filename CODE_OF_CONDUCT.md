# Code of Conduct

ThunderLoad is a small open-source project, and everyone who takes part in
it — reporting issues, opening pull requests, reviewing, or discussing —
is expected to treat the other people here decently.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing about a
  technical choice. Criticise the code or the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith. If a message reads as hostile, ask what was meant
  before answering in kind.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of harassment, discrimination, and
  personal attacks, including slurs and unwelcome sexual content.
- Do not publish anyone's private information, and do not use the project
  to spam, advertise, or push unrelated products.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, intimidation, threats, discriminatory language, sustained
disruption of discussion, and any attempt to use the project to harm or
deceive its users are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the repository owner — contact the maintainer privately
through their GitHub profile — and the report will be handled
confidentially. The maintainer may take any of these steps, depending on
severity: a private warning, a temporary restriction from participating, or a permanent ban from the project.
Decisions about enforcement are made by the repository owner and are final.
